India's Semiconductor Boom: Bengaluru Fab Lab Begins Operations

Published on: October 24, 2024 | By Kushal Sharma

The Rise of Domestic Chip Manufacturing

India is rapidly moving beyond software services into hardware and chip design. With multiple state-of-the-art semiconductor fabrication units breaking ground in Gujarat and Karnataka, engineering colleges are now launching specialized VLSI design curriculums.

Impact on College Hiring

Hardware engineering internships saw a 38% increase in tier-1 and tier-2 Indian technical colleges this semester. Students proficient in Verilog, SystemC, and embedded firmware are in high demand.
