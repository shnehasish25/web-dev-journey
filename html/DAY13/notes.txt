Day 13 - Semantic HTML5 Architecture

Today's focus: Replacing "div soup" with meaningful semantic elements.

Learnings:
1. "Div Soup" is when beginners build websites like:
   <div class="header">
     <div class="nav-bar">
       <div class="menu-item">...</div>
     </div>
   </div>
   This gives ZERO meaning to browsers, search engines, or blind users.
2. Semantic HTML5 tags:
   - <header>: Introductory content or logo banner.
   - <nav>: Primary navigational links.
   - <main>: Central, unique content of this specific webpage (only ONE <main> per HTML document!).
   - <article>: Complete, self-contained piece of content (blog post, news article, forum comment).
   - <section>: Thematic group of content with a heading.
   - <aside>: Content tangentially related to the main article (sidebar, author bio, trending topics).
   - <footer>: Author, copyright, back to top links.
3. Rule of thumb: If the content makes sense standalone (like on an RSS reader), use <article>. If it's a thematic group within a page, use <section>.
