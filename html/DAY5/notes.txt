Day 5 - Audio & Video in HTML5

Today's focus: Native media players without Flash plugin!

Learnings:
1. Back in the day, websites required Adobe Flash player to play media. HTML5 introduced native <video> and <audio> tags.
2. <video> tag attributes:
   - controls: Displays play, pause, volume, full screen buttons.
   - autoplay: Browser generally blocks autoplay WITH sound because it annoys users.
   - muted: If combined with autoplay, modern browsers will allow video to autoplay!
   - loop: Video replays automatically when finished.
   - poster: Thumbnail image shown before the video starts playing.
3. Fallback text: Always put text between <video>...</video> for ancient browsers that don't support HTML5.
4. <audio> tag works similarly with controls and source tags.
