Day 12 - HTML5 Form Validation & Regex Patterns

Today's focus: Native client-side validation attributes.

Learnings:
1. Before HTML5, you had to write 30 lines of JavaScript just to check if an email was valid. HTML5 does this automatically in the browser!
2. Key validation attributes:
   - required: Field cannot be submitted empty.
   - min and max: Number or date bounds.
   - minlength and maxlength: String character counts.
   - pattern: Regular expression (regex) test!
3. Regular Expressions practiced for Indian inputs:
   - Indian 10-digit mobile number: `[6-9][0-9]{9}` (Must start with 6, 7, 8, or 9 and have 10 digits total!).
   - Indian Postal PIN Code: `[1-9][0-9]{5}` (6 digits, cannot start with 0!).
   - Permanent Account Number (PAN Card): `[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{1}` (5 letters, 4 numbers, 1 letter).
4. Date and Time inputs:
   - type="date", type="time", type="datetime-local".
   - You can restrict dates: min="2024-01-01" max="2024-12-31".
