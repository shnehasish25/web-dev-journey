Day 17 - HTML Entities, Symbols & Emojis

Today's focus: Special characters that cannot be typed directly in HTML.

Learnings:
1. Why HTML Entities exist:
   - Characters like `<` and `>` are reserved by HTML for tags! If you type `5 < 10`, the browser might think `< 10` is an unclosed tag!
   - To show literal angle brackets, we use entities:
     * `&lt;` = Less than (<)
     * `&gt;` = Greater than (>)
     * `&amp;` = Ampersand (&)
     * `&quot;` = Quotation mark (")
2. Indian Rupee Symbol:
   - Character: ₹
   - Entity: `&#8377;`
   - Super useful on Indian ecommerce portals (Flipkart, Amazon India, Zomato)!
3. Non-Breaking Space:
   - `&nbsp;`: Creates a space that guarantees two words will NEVER break onto separate lines (e.g. `₹&nbsp;500` or `10&nbsp;kg`).
4. Other useful entities:
   - `&copy;` = Copyright symbol (©)
   - `&reg;` = Registered trademark (®)
   - `&trade;` = Trademark (™)
   - `&bull;` = Bullet point (•)
   - `&rarr;` / `&larr;` / `&uarr;` / `&darr;` = Directional arrows (→ ← ↑ ↓)
