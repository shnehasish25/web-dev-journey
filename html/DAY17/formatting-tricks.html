The Magic of &nbsp; (Non-Breaking Space)

Problem:

When a screen gets very narrow on mobile phones, sometimes words split awkwardly. For instance, having "₹" at the end of line 1 and "500" pushed down to line 2 looks super unprofessional!

Solution:

Use &nbsp; between them:

Special Biryani Price: ₹ 350

Bag Weight: 15 kg

Meeting Time: 10 PM

The browser will treat "₹ 350" as a single glued unit that always moves together!
