Day 6 - Iframes & External Embeds

Today's focus: <iframe> (Inline Frame).

Learnings:
1. <iframe> embeds another HTML document inside your current page!
2. Practical use cases:
   - Embedding tutorial videos and study music.
   - Embedding Google Maps for a college or business address.
   - Embedding Spotify playlists or CodePen demos.
3. Security considerations:
   - 'sandbox' attribute limits what the embedded page can do (stops malicious scripts from popping alerts or reading cookies).
   - 'loading="lazy"' stops the iframe from downloading until the user scrolls near it (huge performance boost!).
4. Many websites like Google.com or Facebook send 'X-Frame-Options: DENY' header, meaning they intentionally forbid being loaded inside an iframe to prevent clickjacking attacks!
