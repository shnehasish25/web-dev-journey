Engineering College Admissions - Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the minimum KCET / COMEDK cutoff for Computer Science?

For general category students, the cutoff for CSE typically ranges between rank 1,200 to 3,500 depending on the college tier. Information Science and AI/ML branches usually close around rank 5,000.

2. Do I need an expensive high-end gaming laptop for coding?

Absolutely not! Any laptop with at least 8GB or 16GB RAM, an Intel i5 / AMD Ryzen 5 processor, and a 512GB SSD is more than enough for web development, Python, C++, and database labs. You do NOT need a ₹1,50,000 GPU for college.

3. Is hostel accommodation provided on campus?

Yes, on-campus hostels with Wi-Fi, laundry facilities, and south/north Indian mess food are available on a first-come, first-served basis during admission counseling.

4. What programming languages should I learn before semester starts?

Starting with basic C or Python syntax is great. But jumping early into HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript gives you an immediate head start for technical club hackathons!
