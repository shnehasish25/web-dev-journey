Day 9 - Advanced Tables (Colspan & Rowspan)

Today's focus: <thead>, <tbody>, <tfoot>, colspan, and rowspan.

Learnings:
1. `colspan="n"`:
   - Expands a cell HORIZONTALLY across n columns.
   - Example: Merging lunch break across Monday to Friday.
2. `rowspan="n"`:
   - Expands a cell VERTICALLY across n rows downwards.
   - CAUTION: This is the trickiest concept in HTML!
   - When you put `rowspan="2"` on a cell in row 1, remember that in row 2, that spot is already taken! 
     If you write the same number of <td> tags in row 2, everything gets pushed out to the right and ruins your table layout!
3. Semantic table tags:
   - <thead>: Header section
   - <tbody>: Main rows
   - <tfoot>: Summary row at the bottom

- Indian Railways Vande Bharat timetable with merged station stops.
- College weekly timetable with labs spanning multiple hours.
