☕ Kushal's Kadak Masala Chai Recipe

The ultimate fuel for late-night debugging sessions. Serves 2 cups.

Ingredients Checklist (<ul>)

  • Water: 1 ½ cups
  • Milk (Full cream): 1 cup
  • Strong Assam Black Tea powder: 2 ½ teaspoons
  • Sugar: 2 teaspoons (adjust to taste)
  • Chai Masala Spices:
    • Fresh Ginger (adrak): 1 inch, grated or crushed
    • Green Cardamom (elaichi): 2 pods, crushed
    • Cloves (laung): 2 pieces
    • Black Peppercorn (kali mirch): 2 pieces, crushed (for throat kick!)

Step-by-Step Brewing Instructions (<ol>)

  1. Add 1 ½ cups of water to a saucepan and bring it to a rolling boil on medium heat.
  2. Add the freshly crushed ginger, cardamom, cloves, and black pepper. Let it simmer for 2 minutes to extract the aromatic oils.
  3. Add 2 ½ teaspoons of Assam tea powder and let the mixture boil until the water turns deep red.
  4. Pour in 1 cup of milk and add sugar.
  5. Bring the chai to a boil, then reduce heat when the foam rises. Repeat this 3 times (the secret to rich dhaba-style chai!).
  6. Strain the piping hot chai into clay kulhads or glasses.
  7. Serve immediately with hot Parle-G biscuits or spicy samosas!
