Day 291 - CRUD: updateOne, updateMany & $inc

Topic Overview:
Modifying documents with $set, $inc, $unset, and the upsert flag.

Keep an eye on:
- Always use update operators like $set! If you pass a plain object to updateOne without $set, it replaces the entire document!
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
