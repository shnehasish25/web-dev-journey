Day 287 - MongoDB Architecture, mongosh & ObjectId

Topic Overview:
Introduction to MongoDB, BSON binary JSON format, mongosh shell commands, and ObjectId anatomy.

Keep an eye on:
- ObjectId is a 12-byte BSON type: 4-byte timestamp, 5-byte random value, 3-byte incrementing counter. You can extract timestamp using doc._id.getTimestamp().
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
