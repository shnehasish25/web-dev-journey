Day 293 - CRUD: deleteOne, deleteMany & Soft Deletes

Topic Overview:
Removing documents from collection and implementing soft deletion.

Keep an eye on:
- In production, prefer soft deletion ($set: { isDeleted: true, deletedAt: new Date() }) so historical invoices and analytics aren't orphaned.
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
