Day 295 - Data Modeling: Embedding vs Referencing

Topic Overview:
Deciding when to embed subdocuments (1-to-Few) vs referencing ObjectIds (1-to-Many).

Keep an eye on:
- Rule of thumb: Embed if data is frequently read together and bounded in size (e.g. delivery addresses). Reference if data grows indefinitely (e.g. user orders over 5 years).
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
