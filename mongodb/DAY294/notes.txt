Day 294 - Indexes & Query Performance (COLLSCAN vs IXSCAN)

Topic Overview:
createIndex(), compound indexes, unique constraints, and using explain('executionStats').

Keep an eye on:
- A query without an index performs a COLLSCAN (scans every single document in collection!). With an index, it performs an IXSCAN (lightning fast B-tree lookup).
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
