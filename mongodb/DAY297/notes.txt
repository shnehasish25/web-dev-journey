Day 297 - Aggregation Framework Part 2: $group & Accumulators

Topic Overview:
Grouping data with $group and calculating $sum, $avg, $min, $max.

Keep an eye on:
- $group takes an _id expression representing the grouping key. Use null to aggregate across the entire collection into a single summary document.
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
