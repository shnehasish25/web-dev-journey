Day 292 - Updating Arrays: $push, $pull & $addToSet

Topic Overview:
Manipulating array fields in documents without replacing the whole array.

Keep an eye on:
- $push adds duplicates; $addToSet adds an element ONLY if it does not already exist in the array (great for tags and categories).
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
