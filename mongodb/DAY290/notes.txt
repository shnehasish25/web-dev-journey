Day 290 - Projections, Sorting & Pagination

Topic Overview:
Projection inclusion/exclusion, ascending (1) and descending (-1) sort, skip and limit.

Keep an eye on:
- You cannot mix inclusion and exclusion in a single projection, with the sole exception of the _id field (e.g. { name: 1, price: 1, _id: 0 }).
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
