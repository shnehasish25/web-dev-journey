Day 296 - Aggregation Framework Part 1: $match & $project

Topic Overview:
Building data processing pipelines with $match, $project, $sort, and $limit stages.

Keep an eye on:
- Always place $match and $sort at the very start of the pipeline so MongoDB can leverage database indexes to filter data early!
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
