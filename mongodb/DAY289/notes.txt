Day 289 - CRUD: Query Filters & Logical Operators

Topic Overview:
find() and findOne() with $gt, $gte, $in, $and, $or comparison operators.

Keep an eye on:
- $in takes an array of target values. An explicit $and is only needed when testing the same field multiple times, otherwise comma separation acts as implicit AND.
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
