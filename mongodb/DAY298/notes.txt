Day 298 - Aggregation Framework Part 3: $lookup & $unwind

Topic Overview:
Left outer joins across collections with $lookup and flattening arrays with $unwind.

Keep an eye on:
- $lookup outputs joined documents as an array. Use $unwind to flatten the array into individual document records for easier property access.
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
