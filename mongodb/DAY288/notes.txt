Day 288 - CRUD: insertOne & insertMany

Topic Overview:
Inserting single and multiple BSON documents, write concerns, ordered vs unordered batch inserts.

Keep an eye on:
- By default, insertMany is ordered: if document #3 fails validation, operations halt and remaining docs are NOT inserted. Pass { ordered: false } to continue inserting the rest.
- Test queries directly in mongosh or MongoDB Compass.
- Always check executionStats to avoid unindexed full collection scans.
