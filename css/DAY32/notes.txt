Day 32 - CSS Grid Part 2 (Areas & Responsive Auto-Fit)

Today's focus: Advanced Grid & The Holy Grail Responsive Grid.

Learnings:
1. `grid-template-areas`:
   - You can name sections like an ASCII art map:
     "header header"
     "sidebar main"
     "footer footer"
   - In child elements: `grid-area: header;`
   - Super readable and intuitive!
2. The Holy Grail Responsive Grid (NO MEDIA QUERIES NEEDED!):
   `grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(250px, 1fr));`
   - Automatically rearranges columns based on available space!
   - Drops from 4 columns to 3 to 2 to 1 column smoothly on phones!
