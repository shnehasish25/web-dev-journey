Day 27 - Positioning (Static, Relative & Absolute)

Learnings:
1. position: static (default browser flow).
2. position: relative:
   - Nudges element with top/left/right/bottom without affecting surrounding elements.
   - CRITICAL: Acts as the coordinate anchor for any child with position: absolute!
3. position: absolute:
   - Taken completely out of document flow.
   - Positions relative to nearest positioned ancestor (an ancestor with position other than static).
   - Real world use: Discount tags on product cards (Swiggy / Amazon 50% OFF badge in top-right corner)!
