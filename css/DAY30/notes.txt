Day 30 - Flexbox Part 2 (Items, Growth & Wrapping)

Learnings:
1. `flex-wrap: wrap`: Essential for responsive card layouts! Without wrap, flex items squish infinitely and spill off screen.
2. `flex-grow: 1`: Item grows to occupy remaining empty space.
3. `flex-shrink: 1`: Item shrinks when screen gets narrow.
4. `flex-basis`: Default starting size before growing/shrinking.
5. Shorthand `flex: 1`: Sets grow to 1, shrink to 1, basis to 0. All cards share equal width!
