Day 28 - Fixed & Sticky Positioning + Z-Index

Learnings:
1. position: fixed:
   - Pinned relative to viewport window.
   - Stays on screen even when scrolling down 5000px!
   - Perfect for: Floating WhatsApp support button, bottom mobile navigation.
2. position: sticky:
   - Hybrid! Acts like normal relative element until you scroll past its threshold (e.g. top: 0), then sticks like fixed!
   - Great for: Sticky table headers, sticky navbar.
3. z-index:
   - Controls 3D layer depth (stacking order).
   - Higher number sits on top of lower number.
   - NOTE: z-index ONLY works on elements with a position property (relative, absolute, fixed, sticky)!
